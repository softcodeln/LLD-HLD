****LLD Important Point****:
In Clarify Requirements step you ask how the system should behave in edge cases.You don't solve them there.
What you do with an edge case {Ask about it, or state your assumption, and write down the expected behaviour}
Concurrency expectations: what must never happen, e.g. two people booking the same seat

****How to make Design Pattern Visible in LLD Solution****:

1) Creational Design Patterns

2) Structural Design Patterns

A.Facade Design Pattern
Keep the logic in the subsystems; the facade only calls them in order
Make the facade the only door for Client to enter and dont't let Controllers call subsystems directly and the facade.

3) Behavioural Design Patterns

B. Observer Design Pattern
1.Name the subject, the interface, the concrete observers, and the notify line
2.Show 2–3 Observers, to prove they're swappable
3.Pass an event object with ids, type and timestamp like onMessage(event, id, current_Time()), so the observer can act on it.
